Phonon-2 weights in the phonon-2-c format

These files are the weights of FermionResearch/Phonon-2 re-encoded for the
phonon-2-c inference engine (https://github.com/eschmidbauer/phonon-2-c). They
are distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
licence (CC-BY-4.0), the licence of both models they derive from. See
LICENSE-WEIGHTS-CC-BY-4.0.txt. The code that rebuilds them is licensed
separately, see CODE below.

================================================================================
CHANGES MADE HERE
================================================================================

Relative to the Phonon-2 release (phonon-2.bps.tar.zst, container
model.fermion, sha256
4b6bfa3a12cc3c4e0a54f2ab3ec4ca7a842b09e5c7ecfc8e7ca0ac6cc8c11468), the
weights were re-encoded by phonon-2-c's convert_weights.py into its flat
phonon-2-c-weights-v1 format:

  - the five-value encoder linears are kept as five values (2-bit sign and
    1-bit lo/hi planes with the fp32 lo/hi per output column);
  - the 6-bit integer tables are widened to int8 with fp32 scales (exact);
  - fp16 tensors are widened to fp32 (exact);
  - the depthwise convolution's BatchNorm is folded into its weights and bias,
    computed in double precision and stored as fp32 (the one rounding step).

No value was re-trained or re-quantised.
